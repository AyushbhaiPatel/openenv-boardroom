For people who work with AI agents

Let your agent remind you.

Claude Code, Cursor, or any MCP host can create escalating reminders for you. Tell your agent “call me on Friday at 9am if I have not renewed the domain” and it schedules the whole chain through OpenBoardroom.

The reminder fires from our servers, not from the agent. Close the laptop, end the session, go offline. The phone still rings.
